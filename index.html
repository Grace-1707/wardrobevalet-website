About

Grace Webb

The Wardrobe Valet started with a simple idea: sorting a wardrobe shouldn't mean a guilt trip to the charity shop, or a pile that sits in the hallway for months.

I visit homes across Bedfordshire, Hertfordshire and Cambridgeshire to edit, organise, and pay a fair price for whatever you're ready to let go — a friendly hand through the edit, start to finish.
